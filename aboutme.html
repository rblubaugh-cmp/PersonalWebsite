About Me

I’m a Cleveland-born graphic designer who traded in artboards for code editors. After 15 years of designing for international clients like Disney, Chef Gordon Ramsay, the Vanderpumps, and Regent Seven Seas Cruises, I jumped at an opportunity from my employer to pivot into a coding role—and I'm excited for what my future will bring.

I love bridging the gap between sharp visual design and solid development. Outside of work, life is a mix of raising a teenager, playing bass, and cheering on Cleveland sports and the US Rugby 7s team. I’m also a lifelong gamer (big on sports titles and extraction shooters) who recently started collecting vintage games and consoles. Throw in a Pokémon card habit—topped off by my prized Van Gogh Pikachu—and my free time is officially spoken for.
